Links in a note are typed, readable, and reachable: `[text](url)` and the
`@@path/to/file` shorthand now make one as you type, a link follows the card's
own ink instead of a fixed colour that was unreadable on a filled card, and the
hover preview no longer prints the path in the card's ink on the plane's dark
chrome. A card's footer also says when the note was last saved, and the close
toast names the file once.
