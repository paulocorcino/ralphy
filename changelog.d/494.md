headline: 🔒 **Daemon security** — access token on 127.0.0.1, owner-only files on Windows
---
The daemon can ask for its access token also on 127.0.0.1 (`ralphy daemon
require-token on`), and on Windows its token and stores are now readable by your
account only. `ralphy daemon setup --name --avatar` sets the name without
questions.
