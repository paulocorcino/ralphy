A note card is written in a hand and a size of your choosing — sans, serif or
mono, in five steps — chosen from the palette and kept in the file, so a note
comes back in its own hand when you open it again. The file's own menu moved
out of the card's titlebar into a gear in the footer, beside the path it acts
on, and hiding a note is the eye's job alone now.
