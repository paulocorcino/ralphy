---
kind: feature
headline: ▥ **Fence as columns** — open every console of a fence side by side in one click
---
A new button in the fence title bar opens all of the fence's consoles as
columns. The grid follows their places on the stage: consoles side by side
become columns, and consoles one above the other become rows of one column.
Restore each console to put it back where it was.
