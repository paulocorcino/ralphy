topic: rows-in-columns
headline: 🧱 **Rows in columns** — open a console below another one, not only beside it
---
Columns can now hold consoles one above the other. The title bar button is now
"Slice": pick Right to open a new column, or Down to open the console
below the one you are in. Alt+Shift+↑/↓ moves between the rows of a column.
