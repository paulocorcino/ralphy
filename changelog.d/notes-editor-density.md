A note card is a card, not a page. Its editor's toolbar and slash menu are
sized for a 320 px note instead of a full document and their icons are drawn in
a colour you can see; bullets, numbers and task checkboxes appear at all again;
the `+`/`⠿` block handle that had nowhere to sit is gone (`/` opens the same
menu); a click on the blank space below the last line keeps the caret instead
of dropping the next keystrokes; and pressing Enter reads as the next line
rather than a gap. A note's title now lives in the file's header instead of
being repeated as a heading at the top of the body — existing notes keep their
name and are migrated the first time you rename one. Renaming a note no longer
makes the browser offer to save the title as a password, and a mermaid diagram
that does not parse no longer leaves its error cartoon on the plane.
